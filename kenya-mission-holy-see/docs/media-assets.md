## Required handling

For every approved photograph, record the original source page, photographer, copyright or usage status, usage restrictions, required attribution, date obtained and an accurate image description. Optimise only dimensions and formats; do not alter factual content or conceal required attribution.

Do not publish a photograph if provenance or reuse rights cannot be verified.
